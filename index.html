▣ MirrorBridge

No installation required

Share an iPhone screen to a Chromebook using browser-to-browser WebRTC, where supported. No account or backend.

Chromebook viewer

Open this page on your Chromebook and create an offer. Then open the same page on your iPhone, select Share, and paste the offer there.

iPhone sender

Use Safari on an iPhone with browser screen-capture support. Screen sharing requires HTTPS and explicit permission. Camera mode shows what the camera sees, not the iPhone screen.

Ready. Choose your device above.

Pairing is manual: copy the offer and answer between devices using a method you trust. No pairing text is uploaded to this site. WebRTC may use public STUN servers to discover network paths. Some school networks block peer connections.
